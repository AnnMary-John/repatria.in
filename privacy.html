Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains how Repatria Private Limited ("Nock", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use Repatria mobile apps, website and related services (the "Platform"). It applies to everyone who uses Nock, whether you request tasks or complete them.

  • Client: a person who requests a task through Nock, by chatting with Ally or by choosing a task category.
  • Runner: a verified individual who accepts and completes tasks through Nock.
  • Ally: Nock's AI assistant, which helps create, clarify and coordinate tasks in English and Malayalam.

We process personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under them. By creating an account and giving consent where asked, you agree to the processing described here. Where a section applies only to Clients or only to Runners, it says so.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the service: create tasks, match Clients with suitable Runners, show progress, confirm completion and process payments and payouts.
  • To verify Runners: confirm identity and eligibility before a Runner can accept tasks, and re-verify when required.
  • For safety and trust: detect fraud, prevent misuse, investigate incidents and disputes, and enforce our Terms.
  • To communicate with you: send task updates, OTPs, receipts, support replies and important account notices.
  • To improve Nock: understand how the Platform is used, fix errors and improve matching, Ally and task categories. We use aggregated or de-identified data for this where we can.
  • For marketing: send offers or updates only where you have opted in. You can opt out at any time.
  • To meet legal obligations: keep tax and accounting records, respond to lawful requests and comply with applicable law.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Ally, our AI assistant

Ally reads what you type or say so it can turn your request into a structured task, ask follow-up questions and suggest a category or price.

  • Your messages to Ally are processed by our systems and by AI service providers who work on our behalf under contract. They may process data outside India (see Section 8).
  • Our providers are not permitted to use your conversations to train their own general-purpose models.
  • We may review conversations, or use de-identified samples, to improve Ally's accuracy and safety.
  • Ally can make mistakes. Check the task details before you confirm a request.
  • Do not share passwords, full card numbers, OTPs or Aadhaar numbers with Ally.

5. Location data

Location is central to how Nock works, and we use it differently for each side.

  • Clients: we use your location, or the addresses you enter, to create tasks, check whether we serve your area and show nearby Runner availability. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Runners: we collect your location while you are online and while a task is in progress, including when the app is in the background. We use it to offer nearby tasks, share live progress with the Client, calculate distance and earnings, and investigate safety incidents or disputes. We stop collecting it when you go offline.

You can turn off location permission in your device settings. Clients can still enter addresses manually. Runners cannot receive or complete tasks without location access.

Service providers. We share data with companies that help us run Nock, under contracts that limit their use of it:

  • cloud hosting and storage
  • payment gateways and payout processors
  • identity and background verification partners
  • AI providers that power Ally
  • SMS, OTP, masked-calling and push-notification services
  • maps and location services
  • analytics and crash reporting

Legal and safety. We may disclose data to government or law enforcement authorities when required by law, or when needed to protect the safety of a Client, Runner or the public.

Business changes. If Nock is merged, acquired or restructured, personal data may transfer to the new entity, which must keep protecting it under this policy.

7. Payments

Client payments and refunds are handled by our payment gateway partner, Cashfree Payments. Card, UPI and bank details you enter at checkout go directly to the gateway; we receive only the transaction reference, amount and status.

Runner payouts are sent to the bank account or UPI ID the Runner provides. We keep earnings and payout records, and PAN where required, to meet tax and accounting obligations.

8. Storage, security and transfers

Your data is stored primarily on servers located in India (AWS Mumbai region). Some service providers, including AI and messaging providers, may process data outside India. Any such transfer follows Indian law and any restrictions notified by the Central Government.

We protect data with encryption in transit and at rest, access controls limited to staff who need it, logging and monitoring, and regular security reviews. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

10. Your rights and choices

Under Indian law you have the right to:

  • get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we process it
  • correct, complete or update your data
  • ask us to erase your data, subject to legal retention requirements
  • withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing already done, but some features may stop working
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated
  • raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India if it is not resolved

You can update most details in the app under Profile, or write to contact@repatria.in. We will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity first.

You can also turn off marketing messages in the app and change location, camera, microphone and notification permissions in your device settings.

11. Children

You must be 18 or older to create a Nock account as a Client or Runner. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete it. A Client may request a task that involves a child, such as a school pickup, but remains responsible for that request and must share only the information the task needs.
